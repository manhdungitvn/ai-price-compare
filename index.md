<div><h2>Private by design. Powered by local AI.</h2><p class="sub">The AI is <b>Gemini Nano, built into Chrome</b>, running 100% on your device. Your searches go straight from your browser to the stores — exactly like searching there yourself. Everything except the AI verdict works even without Gemini Nano.</p><p><a href="{{ '/PRIVACY' | relative_url }}">Read the privacy policy →</a></p></div>
<ul class="checks">
<li>No server, no account, no analytics — not even on this page.</li>
<li>Nothing is searched and no background tab is opened until you click.</li>
<li>Affiliate links are off by default. If you turn them on, every place a tagged link can be clicked says so, and the price you pay does not change.</li>
<li>It never rewrites links on store pages and never replaces someone else's referral code.</li>
</ul>
