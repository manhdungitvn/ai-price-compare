# AI Reviewer & Price Compare

Chrome extension that compares one product across e-commerce stores (Vietnam & United States) with on-device AI. No API key, no server — your data stays on your device.

![Side panel](screenshot.png)

- 🛒 Stores — Vietnam: Tiki, Thế Giới Di Động, Điện Máy Xanh, CellphoneS, FPT Shop, Siêu Thị Chợ Lớn, Lazada, Shopee, TikTok Shop · US: Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, eBay
- 🏆 Best overall / best price / best reviewed / best selling / best after-sales policy, with a plain-language verdict
- 🎁 Store promotions on every result, 🎟️ coupon wallet tried automatically at checkout (never places an order)
- 💸 "Cheaper at other stores" card right on the product page, 🔔 price-drop alerts
- 🔒 AI runs locally in Chrome (Gemini Nano); rule-based fallback without it
