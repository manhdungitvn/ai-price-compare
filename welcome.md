<h1>You're all set. <span class="grad">Three steps</span> to your first comparison.</h1>
<p class="lead">Chrome hides new extensions behind the puzzle icon, so here is where to find it.</p>

<ol class="steps">
<li><b>Pin the icon.</b> Click the puzzle icon 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin 📌 next to <i>AI Reviewer &amp; Price Compare</i>.</li>
<li><b>Open the side panel.</b> Click the icon, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>. A short setup asks where you shop: we picked a region from your browser, change it there if it's wrong.</li>
<li><b>Compare something.</b> Type a product name in the panel, or open a product page on Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy or eBay and click the floating <b>💸 Find a better price</b> button.</li>
</ol>

<div class="card"><h3>AI is optional</h3><p>Verdicts and review summaries use Gemini Nano, built into Chrome. The panel offers a one-click <b>Enable AI</b> (one-time ~2 GB download). Price comparison, promotions and price alerts work without it.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>On a Chromebook?</h3><p>Most Chromebooks can't run Gemini Nano yet (Chromebook Plus models can). Everything else works the same, and verdicts use built-in rules instead.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Nothing leaves your device</h3><p>No account, no server, no analytics. Searches go straight from your browser to the stores, like searching there yourself. <a href="{{ '/PRIVACY' | relative_url }}">Privacy policy</a></p></div>
